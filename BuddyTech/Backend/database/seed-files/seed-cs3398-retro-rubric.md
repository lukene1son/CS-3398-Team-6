# Sprint 3 Retrospective: Grading Rubric

Turn in one PDF per team (one page is enough).

| Part | Points | What earns full credit |
|---|---|---|
| What went well | 3 | Three specific things, with an example of each |
| What to improve | 4 | Two problems, each with an owner and a concrete change for Sprint 4 |
| Velocity | 2 | Planned vs. completed story points, and one sentence on the gap |
| Team sign-off | 1 | Every team member's name at the bottom |

**Total: 10 points.** Late write-ups lose 1 point per day.
